# 03: Home: weight

**What to build:** Home's weight block: today's weigh-in as the main number, the 7-day trend second, the weekly rate coloured against the target band, and a 14-day trend line. Its detail: a 4- or 12-week chart with weigh-ins, trend and the band shaded, recent weigh-ins with outliers marked, waist values, and this week's predicted vs actual weight. All as of the shown day.

## Comments

- **Read models** (`coach/src/fa_coach/features/web_weight.py`): Home part `weight` (`@registry.view_part("home", "weight", schema="web_home_weight", order=20)`): `weigh_in` = the latest weigh-in on or before the day `{date, kg, outlier}` (the block shows it big only when its date is the day, else "–" with "Last 81.6 kg · Tue 10 Nov"), `weekly_rate_kg` = 7-day trend (`phases.trend_weight`, outliers out) minus the trend 7 days earlier, `vs_band`, and `series` = the trend over the 14 days ending the day. No trend value and no band on Home (per 01). Detail view `home/weight?day=&weeks=4|12` (default 4; anything else 400): weigh-in, trend, rate, `vs_band`, `band`, `series` (weigh-in with outlier flag, trend, band corridor per day), `recent` (last 14 weigh-ins, newest first, outliers marked), `waist` (values up to the day, newest first, max 12), `prediction` (check-in week holding the day). Schemas `web_home_weight.schema.json`, `web_home_weight_detail.schema.json` + examples.
- **Decisions:**
  - `vs_band` reuses `weekly_facts._band` / `_vs_band` (so it can be `below_limit` / `above_limit` too), judged over the 7 days ending the day; **`not_evaluated` while any of those days is in the glycogen window**, as the weekly check-in does; null before a phase or without a band (e.g. the cut's undecided band). Uses the active phase's params (as `daily_target` does); a past day of an ended phase shows no band.
  - **Rate colours:** green in the band, amber outside the band, red past a limit, uncoloured when not judged. Symmetric so it holds for bulk and cut (the prototype had above = red, below = amber).
  - **Band on the chart** is a weight corridor: it starts at the trend on the first day in the range that is in the active phase and past the glycogen window, then rises by the band's low/high rate per day (per phase week). The detail states the band in words ("in the band, +0.10 to +0.30 kg/wk").
  - **Prediction** = the check-in week holding the day (as the food detail), predicted exactly as `weekly_facts` does (last week's trend + surplus at the week start × 7 / kcal_per_kg) vs the trend on the day ("So far" until the week's last day, then "Actual"); shown neutral (no colour).
  - **Waist** includes the active phase's start value (setup `waist_cm_start`) on its start date unless a stored value shares that date.
  - Outlier weigh-ins: Home says "Outlier · not in the trend"; the chart draws them as hollow red rings clamped to the edge (they don't stretch the scale); the list strikes them through with an "Outlier" tag. The chart's scale is at least 0.6 kg tall so noise doesn't look dramatic. Range switch (`4 weeks` / `12 weeks`) is `?weeks=12` in the URL.
  - Detail chart is its own component (`pages/home/weight-chart.ts`) since the shared `app-line-chart` only shades a flat band; Home uses the shared `app-line-chart`.
- **Tests:** `coach/tests/test_web_home_weight.py`, 17 tests through the Coach seam (worked example of test_weekly_checkin): in band / under / over band / over the limit, glycogen window not judged, no weigh-in on the day, outlier on the day, 14-day line, a past day with later data present, before any phase; detail 4 and 12 weeks with the band corridor, other ranges refused, recent weigh-ins with outliers, waist present (incl. start value) / absent, prediction (end of week and mid-week) / absent.

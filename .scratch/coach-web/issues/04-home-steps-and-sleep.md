# 04: Home: steps and sleep

**What to build:** Home's steps block: a bar of steps vs the step goal with steps to go and the kcal ± so far, and a not-tracked state for days under the not-tracked limit; its detail shows 14 days against the goal. Home's sleep block: last night as hours asleep and from–to ("–" without data), read from `fa-api`'s sleep endpoint; its detail shows 14 nights.

**Blocked by:** 02

- [x] Sleep comes from `fa-api` directly in the frontend (it isn't coach data); a missing night or error shows "–"

- [x] Build + screenshots in Comments; tests green

## Comments

- **Coach read models** (`coach/src/fa_coach/features/web_home_steps.py`): part `home.steps` (order 30) = {tracked, steps, goal, to_go, kcal, run_steps_removed, provisional}; view `home/steps` = the 14 days ending on the day (oldest first, each {date, tracked, steps, kcal, run_steps_removed}) plus goal, average_steps over tracked days, days_tracked, days_at_goal. Both reuse `phases.daily_target` / `phases.day_steps`, so steps = MFP steps minus run steps and kcal = the step part of that day's target, k × (steps − goal), under the targets valid that day. Schemas and examples: `web_home_steps(.schema|.example).json`, `web_home_steps_detail(...)`.
- **Decisions:** on a not-tracked day `steps`, `to_go` and `kcal` are null. The target still counts the day at the window average, but showing that number would look like a deficit (story 31), so the block just says "Not tracked". Before a phase, or before its start, `kcal` is null and the steps still show. The step goal is the current one: setup values keep no history, the same as `daily_target`.
- **Tests** (`coach/tests/test_web_home_steps.py`, 8 tests through the Coach seam, schema-checked): below goal, above goal, not tracked, run day (Strava run steps removed), past day (final, not provisional), before any phase, detail for today (pre-phase days have no kcal, a not-tracked day, average and days at goal), detail for a past day.
- **Sleep** (`web/src/app/pages/home/sleep.ts`, `SleepApi`): `GET /api/sleep?date=` from fa-api, `phone` field only; a missing night or any error returns null and shows "–". Home's block shows the night ending on the day ("7 h 36 · 23:10–06:46") with no notes, and reloads when Home's view reloads (refresh) without flickering. The detail `/home/sleep` makes 14 requests (one per night) and shows the average over nights with data, "N of 14 nights", columns and a list (no verdict colours, since sleep has no target, and no refresh button, since the coach sync doesn't touch sleep).
- **UI:** `pages/home/blocks/steps-block.ts` (as in the 01 prototype: steps big / goal, kcal right (green when positive, gray otherwise), bar green once the goal is reached, "N to go" or "Goal reached"; not tracked: "Not tracked", empty bar, "Goal 10,000"), `blocks/sleep-block.ts`, `steps-detail-page.ts` (`/home/steps`), `sleep-detail-page.ts` (`/home/sleep`). New shared `ui/column-chart.ts` (thin columns, optional dashed reference line, null = faint stub), used by both details. Edited shared files: `home-blocks.ts` (two entries), `home.types.ts` (`steps?` + types), `home.routes.ts` (two routes).

# 06: Week: decision and navigation

**What to build:** The Week tab shows the check-in week (the 7 days the coach judges), the week in progress by default, with arrows to older weeks. The decision comes first in plain words (hold, +100, −200 and why); an open proposal is shown read-only with its reasoning, guardrail status and when it would apply (no approve / reject / counter on the web); an unusable or skipped week is explained in one line; a week in progress shows what's known without a decision. The coach's full weekly report is collapsed at the bottom.

## Comments

- **Read model:** `GET /api/coach/week?week=YYYY-Www` (default: the check-in week holding today), `coach/src/fa_coach/features/web_week.py`, schema `web_week.schema.json` (+ example). The week is named by the ISO week of its last day, as the check-in names it; it ends the day before the check-in weekday (Sunday without one). `state`: `decided` (stored check-in from `weekly_checkin.stored`), `in_progress` (the week holding today: `so_far` = days, weigh-ins, days with food logged, plus `checkin_at`), `awaiting` (over, check-in not run yet), `failed` (failed check-in, nothing changed: "/checkin in Telegram"), `none` (no phase, or the phase started mid-week). The last three carry one `note` line. Navigation: `prev_week` back to the first phase's start, `next_week` null on the week in progress; a future or malformed week is a 400 with a plain reason.
- **Decision:** `outcome` hold/change. It is `change` only when the week's calorie change got past the guardrails (open, approved, rejected or replaced later). A blocked proposal reads as **Hold**, and the blocked proposal is listed under it with code's guardrail lines (which already include the earliest check-in). `why` is the verdict's reasoning. `skip` is one line written by code per skip reason: unusable (weigh-ins/needed, food days logged, stale sync, illness/travel/context note), glycogen window, adherence gate (how far intake was off, or the food days logged), sport load. `confirmation` gives "N of M weeks pointing up/down" (shown only for a counted week that points somewhere).
- **Proposals (read-only):** the proposals based on the week, plus every open proposal on the week in progress. Home's banner (05) links to `/week`, so the open proposal is right there. Each one carries `change` (`proposals.describe_change`), reason, counter/rejection reasons, the guardrail status and details, and when it applies: an open one applies "from the day you approve it", with the average daily target now → after and the MFP goal (`proposals.mfp_goal_after`); an approved one has `applied_on` and before → after from its stored outcome. There is no web command for proposals. On the page, the open status is a quiet pill reading "Waiting in Telegram". There are no buttons and no explanatory text.

The envelope schema doesn't set `additionalProperties: false`, and `web_week.resolve(coach, query, now)` gives a part the week (dates, `is_current`, stored check-in). Blocks render in a `.grid` between the proposals and the collapsed report.
